United States

Premier education, global prestige

Home to iconic Ivy League institutions and top-tier public research universities, the US offers unmatched academic variety. With thousands of accredited colleges, you can easily find a program perfectly aligned with your career ambitions.

Tuition $15,000 – $60,000 / year
Intakes Fall (August) & Spring (January)
Visa Type F-1 Student Visa

Highlights

Top-Ranked Global Institutions

Gain degrees from Ivy League campuses and 200+ top 500 QS-ranked universities.

Extended Post-Study Work Rights

STEM graduates qualify for up to 3 years of OPT (Optional Practical Training).

Scholarships & Assistantships

Access tuition waivers, merit grants, and paid RA/TA roles to offset expenses.

On-Campus Employment

Work up to 20 hours/week during semesters and full-time during official academic breaks.

Unmatched Industry & Alumni Access

Build valuable professional networks across Fortune 500 companies and tech hubs.

Canada

Excellence in education, a place to call home

From world-renowned universities to safe, inclusive communities, Canada provides an exceptional educational experience—backed by post-study work freedom and seamless pathways to permanent residency.

Tuition $15,000 – $45,000 / year
Intakes Fall (Sep), Winter (Jan) & Summer (May)
Visa Type Canadian Study Permit

Highlights

  • Extended Post-Grad Work Rights: Secure up to a 3-year Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) to gain valuable Canadian work experience.
  • Direct PR Pathways: Graduate into streamlined immigration routes, including Express Entry and Provincial Nominee Programs (PNP).
  • Cost-Effective Excellence: Enjoy high-quality, internationally recognized degrees at a lower tuition cost than in the US or UK.
  • Safe & Inclusive Society: Thrives in a welcoming, multicultural environment known for high safety and quality of life.
  • World-Class Institutions: Home to top-ranked universities and industry-aligned colleges.

Popular Programs

Business Administration Information Technology Engineering Health Sciences Hospitality Management

Australia

Leading-edge research, unforgettable living

At the forefront of global innovation, Australia offers cutting-edge academic programs across its prestigious Group of Eight institutions. Gain high-value qualifications, enjoy an enviable lifestyle, and build a pathway toward long-term global success.

Tuition $18,000 – $60,000 / year
Intakes Semester 1 (Feb) & Semester 2 (July)
Visa Type Student Visa (Subclass 500)

Highlights

  • Group of Eight: ANU, Melbourne, Sydney, UNSW, and more
  • Post-Study Work Visa: 2–4 years depending on location
  • High quality of life and student-friendly cities
  • Strong focus on research and innovation
  • Pathway to Australian Permanent Residency

United Kingdom

Centuries of academic tradition, global prestige

The UK is home to some of the world's oldest and most respected universities. With shorter degree durations (3-year undergraduate, 1-year master's), students can achieve globally recognised qualifications efficiently.

Tuition £12,000 – £45,000 / year
Intakes September/October & January
Visa Type Student Visa (Tier 4)

Highlights

  • Oxford, Cambridge, Imperial, UCL, and Russell Group universities
  • 1-year Master's programs — save time and money
  • Graduate Route Visa: 2 years post-study work
  • Rich cultural heritage and diverse student life
  • Strong industry connections in finance, tech, and media

Popular Programs

Business & Finance Law Computer Science Medicine & Life Sciences Arts & Humanities

New Zealand

Safe, welcoming, globally ranked

New Zealand offers a safe and friendly environment for international students, with globally ranked universities, stunning natural landscapes, and a growing economy with excellent post-study work opportunities.

Tuition NZD $22,000 – $45,000 / year
Intakes Semester 1 (Feb) & Semester 2 (July)
Visa Type NZ Student Visa

Highlights

  • 8 universities, all ranked in the top 3% globally
  • Post-Study Work Visa: up to 3 years
  • One of the safest countries in the world
  • Pathway to New Zealand Permanent Residency
  • Strong focus on practical, industry-relevant education

Europe

Affordable tuition, rich culture, global degrees

Europe offers exceptional higher education at a fraction of the cost of English-speaking countries. Countries like Germany, Netherlands, France, and Ireland offer English-taught programs with minimal or no tuition fees.

Tuition €0 – €40,000 / year (varies)
Intakes Winter (Oct) & Summer (April)
Visa Type National Student Visa

Highlights

  • Germany: tuition-free public universities for all students
  • Netherlands: English-taught programs, innovative education
  • Ireland: EU access, English-speaking, tech hub
  • France: prestigious Grandes Écoles and research universities
  • Schengen visa allows travel across 26 European countries
